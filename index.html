THE COMPANY

A cabaret built to move

Body Art Cabaret is an independent ensemble of performers — dancers, aerialists, costumers, and visual artists — who build a new night of theater in every city they play. Rather than touring a single fixed act, the company reworks its show around each venue: a black-box theater gets an intimate hour of classic tease and reveal, while a festival stage might bring aerial rig work, live paint, and a full band.

What stays constant is the craft. Every costume, set piece, and routine is built by hand by the company itself, and every stop on the route gets a show designed specifically for that room.

THE ACTS

One evening, several disciplines

Each performance is assembled from a rotating repertoire, so no two stops on the route look quite the same.

Classic Burlesque

Tease-and-reveal numbers built on old-world glamour, feathered fans, and hand-sewn costume reveals.

Aerial & Circus Arts

Silks, lyra, and rope work woven into the narrative arc of the show rather than staged as separate stunts.

Live Music & Cabaret

A live vocalist or small band anchors the room, trading the recorded-track feel for something built for that night.

Visual Spectacle

Body paint, projection, and hand-built set pieces turn the stage itself into part of the performance.
